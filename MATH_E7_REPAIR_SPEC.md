# E7 Integrity Repair — implementation specification (written before any code change)

Baseline `c15786f`. Each invariant: existing enforcer → demonstrated defect (audit ref) → smallest shared correction → how it applies across families → bypass check → independent test. Reused components are named; the only new modules are small shared helpers (`evidence-binding.ts`, `domain-audit.ts`), not engines.

## A — Claim-bound evidence
- **Existing:** `proof-obligations.ts` (obligation proven by the evidence found on the claim's **line**), `evidence-provenance.ts` (registry/route/reasoner), typed `structuredObligations` (`certification-authority.ts`: claim = `typed step N`, evidence = reasoner label).
- **Defect (S8):** evidence is matched by line number; typed evidence carries no claim identity; a registered label proves any claim on its line.
- **Correction:** `evidence-binding.ts`: `claimIdentity(text)` (digest of the normalised claim: unicode minus/×/÷/spacing/case, no parsing) and `premisesDigest(problem, studentAssumptions)`. Evidence items carry `claimIdentity` + `premises` + mechanism; an obligation is `proven` only if (1) the evidence's identity covers the obligation's claim (equal, or the claim is a normalised sub-span of the evidence's own claim text on that line), (2) its premises digest equals the certifier's premises digest, (3) the mechanism attribution is trusted. Typed: each step's identity = digest of the *submitted step object* (+ typed problem digest), and the step count/alignment between submitted steps and reasoner steps must agree. Any mismatch ⇒ `unsupported` ("evidence establishes a different claim / different assumptions"), never `proven`.
- **Across families:** one function used by every route that builds obligations (natural, adapter-converted, algebra-text, typed).
- **Bypass check:** `standsOnObligations` additionally requires each proven derivation's evidence to be bound; the obligation layer has no other way to mark `proven`.
- **Tests (independent):** property test generating random claims and random evidence (matched / other claim same line / other premises / unregistered / wrong route); synthetic misbinding (`x=1` evidence vs geometry claim) must never be `proven`; mutation of any bound field flips to non-proven.

## B — Uniform domain semantics
- **Existing:** natural route: `e6-cas.domainReqs`, `plainLogArgs`, the singularity screen and `+C`/log rules in `e6-calc.ts` (and `proof-context`); typed route: `restrictions()` recorded only.
- **Defect (S5):** typed antiderivatives/derivatives/definite integrals never audited against domain; divergent `∫₋₁¹x⁻²` verified; `ln x` for `∫1/x` verified.
- **Correction:** `domain-audit.ts` exports the *same* functions the natural route uses (the singularity screen and log-argument rule are moved out of `e6-calc.ts` and re-imported there — one implementation) plus a converter from typed calculus IR to the e6 AST. The typed calculus gate runs it on every claim: a definite integral whose integrand (or antiderivative) is not defined on the closed interval ⇒ non-verified (`unsupported`: the fundamental theorem does not apply); an antiderivative whose own requirements (log argument positivity, denominators) are not implied by the integrand's domain and not among the stated assumptions ⇒ `missing-assumption`. The CAS (`compareExpr`) is also the second opinion for typed rule-form `invalid` (below).
- **Across families:** derivative, indefinite, definite, limit domains for typed + natural; algebra route already enforces extraneous roots (retained); structured kernels receive the same audit where their claims are calculus expressions.
- **Tests:** property test over generated f (monomials, rational with poles inside/outside the interval, logs) comparing typed and natural twins: same verdict class; divergent integrals never verified through any route; every route's twin agrees on `ln x`/`ln|x|`.

## C — Complete premise binding
- **Existing:** `natural-working.ts` already parses `relation, restriction` (comma suffix → `restrictions`); `proof-context.ts` classifies assumptions; algebra checker treats `eq, ineq` statements as a conjunction/system (premise ignored).
- **Defect (S6, S12):** a premise after the equation does not restrict the solution set; "assume x>0" is a student assumption classed "unrelated" on statement problems.
- **Correction:** the statement is parsed by the existing natural parser; its restrictions become *task premises* in the immutable context and the algebra solution-set comparison (final stated solutions and completeness) is made on the premise-restricted domain; a stated solution violating a premise is `invalid`; premises are order-independent (before/after the equation); contradictory premises (empty feasible set) ⇒ `missing-assumption`/unresolved; a student's own assumption is recorded as student-added and cannot discharge a task assumption (probability `needs`).
- **Tests:** premise add/remove/permute metamorphic relations; generated linear/quadratic/radical statements with premises, oracle = brute-force over exact rationals; contradictory premises.

## D — Derivation rather than coincidence
- **Existing:** `claim-grounding.assessRelevance`, `numericGrounding` (E5), `e6-numbers` chain grounding, `core` route search (advisory, 64 % of valid steps routed in the corpora), obligation `grounding` kind.
- **Defect (S1–S4):** a number is "explained" if it is one `+−×÷` of *any* two pool numbers, the pool contains the goal value and `|n|≤2` is free; probability/complex/linear-algebra chains ground only the first member; algebra lines accepted when merely solution-set-equal and containing literals from nowhere; structured kernels compare every step to the final answer.
- **Correction (one shared literal-provenance rule, `claim-grounding.ts`):** every numeric literal a *new* line introduces must be (i) a task number, (ii) an established number (appears in/ is the value of an earlier accepted line), (iii) a constant licensed by the line's own cited/applicable rule (180/360/90 for angle sums, 2 only with an isosceles/bisector/equal-pair premise), or (iv) the value of a visible sub-expression of the same line over (i)–(iii). Removed: goal value as pool member, "any two pool numbers", free `|n|≤2`. Applied to the E5 geometry chains (existing `numericGrounding`), the E6 chains for **every** member (`e6-numbers`), and algebra lines (new use of the same function over previous-line numbers; distributive products such as 3·4=12 are visible sub-expressions). Structured kernels: intermediate steps whose result differs from the final answer are `unverified`-not-`invalid`, and are proven only by a kernel that checks the step. A bare conclusion remains acceptable when entailed by the task (concise answers); it is the *introduced free literal* that blocks.
- **Rule labels (explicit outcomes):** (1) correct transformation + label confirmed by a rule-semantic kernel (geometry theorem citation, `calculus:methods` rule) ⇒ verified; (2) correct transformation + label contradicted by such a kernel ⇒ `invalid` (false claim in the work); (3) correct transformation + unrecognised/unchecked label ⇒ verified, label recorded `uncertified` in the certification record (never silently certified, never a prerequisite); (4) incorrect transformation + any label ⇒ `invalid`. Typed rule-form `invalid` (claim not in the form the rule produces) is re-judged by the exact CAS: equal ⇒ verified (label `form-not-matched`), unequal ⇒ stays `invalid`.
- **Tests:** laundering mutations (replace a derived number by an equal-valued unjustified expression) must never move non-verified → verified; justified rewrites must stay verified; label permutation test; generated geometry/probability/algebra chains with an independent exact oracle (the oracle checks truth only, not grounding).
- **Declared residual:** coincidences among established numbers (e.g. `x=8−4`-style where both operands are established) remain possible; documented.

## E — Parse fidelity
- **Existing:** `natural-working.ts` (list-number stripper line 79), `e6-parse.ts`, `independent-checker` text intake.
- **Defect (S7, S9):** `1.5y=10` loses its integer part; a misread parse (`[F] from a to b`, `d/dx 1/x`) becomes a trusted `invalid`; one unreadable line makes the whole submission unusable.
- **Correction:** the list-number stripper only strips `n.`/`n)` followed by whitespace (not decimals); every parse that feeds a refutation is round-tripped (print(parse(text)) vs normalised source text); a failed round trip ⇒ `unsupported` (never `invalid`); an unreadable line is `unsupported` at that line with the other lines still assessed (dependents unresolved); a small normalisation of common presentational wrappers for already-supported objects (trailing period, ✓, leading "so/hence/therefore", `°` on angles, `from a to b` bracket evaluation) is done in the existing normaliser, not a new parser.
- **Tests:** notation-preserving rewrite metamorphic relation (verdict class invariant), decimal-leading property test, round-trip property test on generated expressions.

## F — Explicit capability boundaries
- **Existing:** `MECHANISMS` registry, `acceptance-gate` levels, `certification-authority` rules.
- **Defect:** typed rule-form `invalid` is final when no route accepts; an objector's `incomplete` is scope-only; a route's weaker reading can accept what a stronger one never saw.
- **Correction:** each registry mechanism declares the claim families it can establish (`families`); an obligation whose claim family is outside its evidence mechanism's declared families is `unsupported`; rule-form `invalid` is relativised to `unsupported` when no accepting route exists; verdict semantics fixed: `invalid` only for a *fully parsed, round-tripped* refutation, parse/interpretation failures are `malformed`/`unsupported`, undetermined readings `ambiguous`/unresolved.
- **Tests:** capability-boundary property test (claims of a family outside a mechanism's declared families never `proven`); verdict-class semantics test (parse failure never `invalid`).

## Stage gates and reporting
After every stage: `bun test packages/learning/src`, 1725-case scan (`phase6/scan.ts`), injection battery, the Phase-6 focused battery, and a diagnostic pass over the exposed 678 holdout (reported as *exposed*; original frozen results untouched). Reported separately: confirmed false acceptances, false rejections, unsupported/malformed counts, exact agreement, cross-route consistency, invariant violations, latency. No tuning to individual holdout cases.
