Count with me

For grown-ups

Pages finished: 0 of 10. "Right first time" means no wrong taps or answers in that round.

SkillRoundsRight first time

Sit with the child for the first few pages: count aloud together, and at the end ask "so how many?" The game makes each page harder after rounds done right first time and easier after mistakes. A glowing hint appears if nothing is tapped for 7 seconds.

What this teaches
  • Count: touch each one once and hear its number. One tap, one number. The last number said is how many.
  • Find: count only the sheep (not the cows), then pick the numeral. Spotting one kind among others, and reading the written number.
  • Compare: are there more sheep or more cows? Counting two groups and knowing which number is bigger.
  • Make: see a numeral, tap that many and stop. Turning a number into an amount.
  • Quick look: up to 5 things flash and hide. Seeing small amounts at a glance without counting.
  • Page 1 counts to 3; page 10 counts to 10. Each tap plays the next note up, so bigger numbers sound higher.
