      Is this how real sound works?

      Everything that makes a sound is shaking (vibrating). A wider shake is louder; a faster shake is a higher note. The number of shakes each second is the frequency, measured in hertz (Hz). Sound travels as a push through air, water or solids, and cannot cross empty space.

      The numbers here come from simple models: a string, a column of air, a drum skin and a jar that a hand pump can never fully empty. Hearing ranges differ between people and animals; these are typical values.
