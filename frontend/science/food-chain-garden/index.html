Caterpillars eat leaves. Birds eat caterpillars.

Follow the food

Plant → caterpillar → bird. Plants make their own food. Caterpillars get food from plants, and these birds get food from caterpillars.

This is one garden food chain, not a forecast. The numbers show a possible pattern. Real birds can find other food and may visit other gardens.
