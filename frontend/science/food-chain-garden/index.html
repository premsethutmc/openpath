Follow the food

Plant → caterpillar → frog or bird. Plants make their own food. Caterpillars eat leaves. Frogs and birds both eat caterpillars, so they share the same food. Joined-up food chains like this make a food web.

Things to try: take away the birds, take away every hunter, or add lots of caterpillars. Birds can fly in from elsewhere, and butterflies can lay new eggs, but frogs cannot come back on their own.

This is a simple model, not a forecast. Real gardens have many more living things, and numbers change in less tidy ways.
