Lift the Rock

A rock is blocking the path. Try the beam.

Near the rockFarther away
Push neededMedium
Hand travelMedium

Push the beam, then try moving the support.

Why does it feel easier?

The support is the pivot. Move it closer to the rock and your side of the beam gets longer. You need less force, but you push farther to lift the same rock the same height.

This is a first-class lever: the pivot is between the rock and your hand.
