The Robot Who Forgot

On the first day of term, Mr. Okafor carried a dusty cardboard box into the classroom. Inside was a small silver robot with one wobbly wheel and a cracked screen.

"This is Bolt," he announced. "He used to help in the library, but he has forgotten almost everything."

Priya raised her hand immediately. She wanted to know whether Bolt could still speak. Mr. Okafor pressed a button on its back, and the robot whirred, blinked twice and said, "Good morning, Tuesday." Everyone laughed, because it was Monday.

Daniel volunteered to read the instruction manual. It was enormous, written in tiny print, and several pages were missing. He discovered that Bolt needed a new battery and a careful update. Priya offered to borrow a screwdriver from the caretaker.

For the rest of the week, the children took turns looking after the robot. Priya polished the screen while Daniel tightened the wobbly wheel. Grace, who usually preferred drawing, designed a colourful poster explaining how to charge Bolt safely.

On Thursday afternoon the update finally finished. Bolt rolled across the carpet, stopped beside Grace and politely asked to borrow a pencil. Then he sorted the reading books into perfect alphabetical order without a single mistake.

Mr. Okafor was astonished. He admitted that he had nearly thrown the robot away. Daniel suggested that Bolt should become the class librarian, and everyone agreed.

At the end of the week, Grace pinned her poster beside the bookshelf. Underneath it, Priya wrote a reminder in neat handwriting: even a forgetful robot can learn again if somebody is patient.
