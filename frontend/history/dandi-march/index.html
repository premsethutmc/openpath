What’s history? What’s imagined?

Gandhi and his fellow marchers left Sabarmati Ashram on 12 March 1930. Gandhi arrived at Dandi on 5 April and broke the British salt law by picking up natural salt the next morning. The march was part of a wider campaign of nonviolent civil disobedience.

Raman and Dev, their dialogue, and your place on the road are imagined. The walk compresses several weeks. Gandhi is shown but given no invented dialogue.
