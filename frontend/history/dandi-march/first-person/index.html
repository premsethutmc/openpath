Archive and reconstruction

The opening uses genuine 1930 Salt March footage from the GandhiServe/Wikimedia Commons compilation, listed there as public domain in India and the US. The score and footsteps are modern. The edit does not identify a precise filming location.

After the cut, you are an imagined local child. The companion, the photographic-style reconstruction (generated illustration), and the synthesized voice are not archival. Gandhi does not speak. Aslali was the march’s first night halt. Gandhi’s opposition to the salt tax included its burden on poor families.
