# 8. Aggregate metrics and denominators

Generated by `scripts/report.py` from `results/metrics-*.json` (pre-registered metrics, `src/metrics.ts`). Ground truth is the synthetic learner's hidden state: a simulator assumption, not a fact about real students.

Denominators are stated in every cell as n/d. They are never pooled across cohorts. **There is no single success rate.**

## Definitions (src/metrics.ts, pre-registered)

- **weak(kind)**: hidden ability < 0.6, or a misconception of that kind with strength ≥ 0.3. **strong(kind)**: ability ≥ 0.9 and no misconception of that kind ≥ 0.1. The competency kinds are two-step, both-sides, brackets and modelling.
- **Diagnosis.** A kind is *identified* when OpenPath delivered an explanation of that kind, or established, resolved or contested a hypothesis on a question of that family, by the journey's end.
  - "Correctly identified" is over kinds that were weak at the start and that OpenPath asked about.
  - "False diagnosis" is over identified kinds that were strong at the start.
  - Misconception accuracy compares OpenPath's established explanations with the simulator misconceptions held at strength ≥ 0.1. Explanations outside the simulator's model (prerequisite competencies) are counted separately.
- **Teaching.** Each explanation OpenPath delivered is judged against the hidden state just before it (from the learner's own instruction log, aligned by order).
  - *appropriate* if that kind was weak; *unnecessary* if it was strong.
  - *repeated ineffective* = a re-delivery of the same explanation after an earlier delivery whose drawn outcome was "none".
- **Mastery.**
  - An authoritative *claim* is status independent, transfer or verified at a visit's end. It is *false* if any competency kind is weak in the hidden state at that moment.
  - A shadow claim is E1 "independently-demonstrated" for a kind; it is false if that kind is weak.
  - *Leakage* = a claim while E1 reports unsupported sub-skills.
  - Method disagreements are over attempts with judged working.
- **Retention.**
  - Cycles are taken from OpenPath's own retention@2 history, counting only those due before the journey's last visit.
  - Recovery targeting = independent, non-retention answers between a failed check and its recovery that share the failed retention answer's kind.
  - *Cross-family recovery* = a completed recovery with no right answer of the failed kind.
  - *Arrived too early* = a visit on the date shown on the topic page with no delayed-check question asked.
- **Bounds.** 16 visits, 120 days, 250 interactions per visit. A journey is "stalled" when OpenPath offers nothing on 3 consecutive visits (doc 9: almost always after OpenPath's own hand-over to a teacher or parent).
