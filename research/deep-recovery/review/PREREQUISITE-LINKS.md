# Prerequisite links flagged by deep-recovery research: for educator review

**Status: PENDING EDUCATOR REVIEW.** Nothing here has been applied. The graph, the content and the curriculum are unchanged.
Deep recovery only follows relations that already exist: `graph.supportsOf` together with the skills named in the failed question's own solution steps. A missing link therefore means recovery can never reach that skill. A wrong mandatory link sends recovery to the wrong skill. No relation ever stops a learner opening a module.

Graph version: `1.0.0+prerequisites@1.1.0+audit@1.1.2+additions@1.1.1+expansion@1.0.2`. Machine-readable list: `flagged-links.json`, produced by `frontend/src/journey/deep-recovery-flags.ts`. Item counts are distinct items in each target module's diagnostic, practice and check pools, plus generated pools.

For each link, decide **accept / reject / change type**. If you accept, give the relation type (support, plausible or mandatory) and one sentence of reasoning. Mark nothing as approved in the content files. A decision is applied later by a separate content change.
