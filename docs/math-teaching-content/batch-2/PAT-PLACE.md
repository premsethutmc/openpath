# PAT-PLACE: What a digit is worth depends on its place

**Status:** review candidate (not approved, not registered, not served). Strands: number.

**Why this pattern.** 10 families in 6 competencies (two-digit, four-digit, decimal place value) model a digit-versus-value error; batch 2 covers the two-digit entry point.

## The one shared strategy

A digit is worth different amounts in different places. In a two-digit number the first digit counts tens and the second counts ones.
'The value of a digit' is how much it is worth (a tens digit is worth that many tens); 'how many tens' is just the count. The same digit answers the two questions differently.
