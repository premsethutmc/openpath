## Explicitly unsupported

- Subjects: Every subject except Mathematics: a textbook in another subject is read and kept, and its outcomes are marked "not supported yet"; nothing is mapped or taught.
- Textbook formats:
  - Scanned PDFs (no text layer): reading them needs OCR, which is not available here, so they are declined.
  - Word, EPUB, image and slide files: not read; text, Markdown and PDF (text layer) are.
  - AI-assisted extraction or mapping: refused unless the owner approves it, with a cost limit and human review.
- Question types:
  - Proofs, written explanations and constructions (marked by a person, not automatically).
  - Drawing or sketching graphs and diagrams.
  - Expressions as answers in generated questions (hand-written modules accept polynomials; generated ones ask for numbers, sets or a choice).
  - Problems outside the supported problem families (they are declined with a reason, never guessed at).

## What "complete module" means

validate@1 (`packages/learning/src/validate.ts`): one to three diagnostic questions; at least one teaching strategy with its own guided and check questions; at least two practice questions; a transfer question; a two-question delayed check; at least two answer formats among the questions answered alone; no question used twice; every question about the module's competency (probes may ask about what it builds on). It is a structural check plus each generated question's independent mathematical check. It is not an educator review.
