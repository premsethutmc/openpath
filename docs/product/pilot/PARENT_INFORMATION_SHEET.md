# OpenPath spelling pilot: information for parents and guardians

**DRAFT. Not approved.** This sheet must be checked by a data-protection reviewer and approved by the host
organisation and its safeguarding lead before it is given to anyone. Words in [brackets] are filled in then.

## What is this?

OpenPath is a new reading and spelling app being developed by [founder / organisation]. We would like to try it with
a small group of children (5 to 10, aged 10 to 12) at [school or centre], with an adult present at all times.

**We are testing the app, not your child.** The questions are drafts. Some may be too hard, unclear or simply wrong,
and that would be the app's fault. Nothing from the pilot is a mark, a grade or a measure of your child, and nothing
is passed to the school as a result.

## What will my child do?

**Three short sessions**, each 20 minutes or less, on different days between [dates]. Each takes place in a quiet
room at [place], on a phone owned by the pilot.

**In each session:**
- Your child answers questions on spelling, grammar and words.
- In the spelling check, the phone says a word aloud. Your child types it.
- If the phone cannot speak, the adult reads the words aloud instead.

**What the adult does:**
- watches the whole time;
- does not help with answers;
- stops the session at once if your child is tired, upset, or wants to stop.

**Before each session** we ask your child whether they want to take part. They can say no, or stop at any time, and
nobody will mind. Your consent never overrides your child's "no".

## What is recorded?

| Recorded | Not recorded |
|---|---|
| A code instead of a name (for example "P07") | Your child's name in the app |
| Every answer, and when it was given | Photos, video or voice recordings of your child |
| Whether the phone or an adult read the words | Anything your child writes in their own words |
| The app's estimates and the plan it suggests | Your child's location |
| The adult's notes on how the app worked (for example "the keyboard covered the button"), without names | Your contact details, in the app |
| *Optional, if you agree below:* your child's spelling level from the teacher's own assessment, to compare with the app | |

**The list linking codes to names** is kept only by [the host organisation], separately from everything else.

## Where is it kept, and who can see it?

[**Device-only set-up, recommended in PILOT_DECISIONS.md:**]

**Where it is stored:**
- The answers are stored only on the pilot phone, in the app. Nothing is sent over the internet while your child
  uses it.
- After each session, the pilot lead copies the record into an encrypted file. The record stays on the locked pilot
  phone until your child's last session, and is then deleted from it.

**Who can see it:** only these people:
- the pilot lead, [name];
- the developer, [name], to check that the app works correctly;
- the reviewing teacher, [name].

**What it is used for:** only to improve the app. It is not sold, published or shared with anyone else.

**If findings are written up,** for example in a short report, no child can be identified from them.

[**If a hosted server is used instead:** the answers are stored on a server in [country/region] run by [provider].
The people who run the server can technically reach stored data. Only the people named above look at pilot
records, and every developer look is logged.]

Yes, at any time and without giving a reason. Tell [contact]. We will:
- stop your child's sessions;
- delete their records and notes;
- confirm the deletion to you in writing.
