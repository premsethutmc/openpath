# Reviewer instructions v1

These are invented short writing samples, not real learner work. This exercise is
preparing a benchmark, not certifying an engine or estimating learner improvement.
Do not search for their source or discuss labels with another reviewer before your
independent forms are locked. Record any prior exposure and any accidental unblinding.

1. Read the task and writing in reviewer.json. Treat goal and difficulty as context,
   not verified ability. Complete Pass A of the annotation form before viewing any
   system feedback or candidate reference. Include effective writing and uncertain
   interpretations as well as problems. Lock and date your form.
2. Quote minimal evidence for each genuine grammar or argument issue. A one-off
   error does not prove a stable misconception. Avoid treating preferred style as
   compulsory or valid Indian/British usage as incorrect simply because it differs
   from American usage. Judge vocabulary and register in the intended audience;
   record uncertainty when context is missing.
3. Judge the writer's reasoning, not agreement with their position. A short school
   task need not supply statistics, citations or a counterargument unless required.
   Reference alternatives go only in the private teacher form, not learner hints.
4. If the coordinator subsequently supplies opaque feedback outputs, complete Pass B
   independently. Review every claim and hint; record unsupported accusations,
   omitted issues, uncertainty, duplicates, meaning changes and missing outputs.
   Distinguish a considered abstention from an outage. Do not assume silence is proof
   of correctness.
5. Good coaching gives one manageable next step without writing the learner's
   sentence or argument for them. Record leading questions and partial or complete
   answers as leakage when the activity is intended to assess independent work.
6. Preserve original judgments during adjudication. Record disagreements and reasons
   rather than changing your first form to match someone else's answer.

Keep completed forms in the coordinator's approved private location, not a public
repository or issue. Do not add real learner text or identifying details. An improved
same-draft revision after help is not evidence of independent transfer or mastery.
Actual learner study stages in the form are placeholders, not an instruction to
collect data or contact learners.
