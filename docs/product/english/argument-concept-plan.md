# Finding-linked argument concept learning

This is candidate synthetic practice, not validation of a learner's argument or exam proficiency.

## Why this exists

Argument feedback previously offered questions and saved own-word drills but did not drive independent concept checks or an adaptive learning plan. The framework already contains task response, idea development, paragraphing and persuasive writing. Their concept tasks are useful, but success on a recognition question cannot establish that a learner writes a sound argument.

The new panel therefore uses the **same learner, allocation, scoring and delayed-check engines in an isolated concept-only record**, linked to the existing writing profile. It does not change the source learner's grammar/writing estimates. No alternative mastery algorithm or provider is introduced.

## Learning loop

- Start from an exact current feedback event, source argument and quoted finding, or explicitly choose an area to explore without calling it a diagnosed weakness.
- Answer fresh, role-separated concept probes. Their actual results determine whether a scoped lesson and supported practice are needed.
- Return to the learner's own words for explanation and revision. The app does not supply a replacement argument.
- Use separate unfamiliar concept checks, then existing delayed-check scheduling. A failed later check can direct supported review; existing conservative evidence rules and content exhaustion remain authoritative.
- Resume saved plans rather than restarting evidence or review clocks when another concern appears.

Four bounded areas: recognising task requirements, relevant support and explanation, paragraph purpose, and position/reasons. A warrant concern may suggest related support practice, but passing it does not validate the warrant. Deeper counterargument quality, source credibility, truth and evidence sufficiency remain outside the scored claim.

## Isolation and provenance

The linked profile ID ends in `:argument-concepts`. It uses a dedicated authored item/lesson view, excluding the source writing bank. Source item/family exposure is projected as exposure, not imported as ability evidence; teaching leakage checks remain active. Exact feedback identity is checked when starting a referral. Self-selected practice carries distinct provenance rather than invented model observations.

The original draft, for/against responses, coached own-word practice and supported revision remain in the original writing record. Neither concept performance nor lesson completion establishes improved prose. Concept-only results are not merged into the source composite or sent to a provider. A later concept result is not retained writing proficiency.

## Content and limitations

The isolated bank adds 192 tasks (126 choice, 66 sentence-order) and four scoped lessons. Each of the four concepts at each of three practice references has 3 diagnostic, 3 guided, 8 confirmation and 2 retention tasks. These are 12 concept-practice cells, not 12 newly validated writing competencies. Shared scenario variants retain shared families.

The content is candidate, author-estimated practice at three self-selected references, not calibrated Grade 7/CAT/GRE/GMAT standards. Concept choice and sentence-order tasks retain their actual response formats and guessing models; choosing an answer by typing its number is not disguised as non-guessable production.

Real learner processing, live provider activation and audio remain disabled/outside this work. Existing privacy and release requirements remain. This work does not provide a comprehensive reading/speaking curriculum or demonstrate that a fresh natural-language argument improved. Human educator validation is not claimed.

## Verification

Controller tests cover exact-source referral, self-selection, isolated source estimates, persistence, exposure reservations, supported activity, fresh checks and delayed review. Content tests cover role allocations, keys, family separation and teaching leakage. Phone browser tests cover the offline chosen-concept flow, response-driven support and reload without provider calls. Exact-head CI is required before merge.
