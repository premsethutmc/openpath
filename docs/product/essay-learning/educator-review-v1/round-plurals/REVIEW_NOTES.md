# Plural questions: what to check, item by item

These are the **author's open questions**, so that each reviewer judges the points most likely to be wrong. They are
not decisions and not recommendations: approve, revise or reject each question on its merits. Use the general criteria
in `../round-1/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md` as well.

## For every question

- **One defensible answer.** Is the expected answer the *only* acceptable one? For typed answers, list every other
  spelling or form you would accept (for example British and American spelling) in `suggested_correction`.
- **The named sub-skill.** Does the question test the sub-skill in its row, and not mainly vocabulary, spelling or
  reading?
- **The proposed use.**
  - A *diagnostic* question should be answerable by a Grade 8 learner who has the skill, near band 5.
  - An *independent* or *transfer* question checks the skill without help.
  - A *transfer* question asks the learner to find and correct the error in a new sentence.
  - A *delayed retention* question is asked at least seven days after the skill was first shown.
- **Difficulty.** Is the proposed band (`difficulty_b`, an expert estimate, not calibrated) about right?
- **Context.** Is the sentence natural Indian English for 12–14-year-olds, culturally appropriate, and free of names or
  details that could identify a real child?
- **Overlap.** Does it give away, or nearly repeat, another question in the set? A learner may meet several of them.

## After the review

Record each decision in your own workbook only. The coordinator reconciles and a third educator adjudicates
disagreements. Approved questions still need a separate content release, which gives each approved version a release
date, before any answer to them can count towards verified mastery.
