## Choosing the competencies

Only three Grade 8 competencies have instructional content in the app: subject–verb agreement (7 lesson parts), present
and past simple (5) and articles (7). The other 13 specified competencies have no lesson, so they cannot be piloted.

## How the matrix is built

Each competency's sequence has eight stages. Questions are taken stage by stage for one learner, through the existing
allocation, so a question counted for one stage is not counted again.

The matrix compares two states:

- **Now:** the bank the app ships, with the pilot's pools honoured.
- **If the supplement is approved:** the 96 proposed questions treated as educator-approved. This is a counterfactual.

A count that falls short of what the stage needs is marked ✗.

## Reading the matrix

**No competency is pilot-ready.** That claim needs all three of these to hold:

- every stage has enough eligible content;
- all of that content is educator-reviewed;
- the essay evaluator (stages 1 and 8) is validated.

None holds today.

**What is short:**

- **Questions now.** Each competency is short of unseen questions between band 5 and 6.5 for the checks:
  - the pilot's confirmation and retention pools were written for lower bands;
  - the band 5–6.5 questions on agreement and articles sit in the diagnosis pool.
- **If the supplement is approved.** All three can complete the sequence **with one later check**. The full allowance
  of three later checks is still short: 4 of 6 questions for agreement and articles, 3 of 6 for present and past simple.
  The owner can either:
  - pilot with one later check (`followUpChecks: 1` for the pilot); or
  - commission more reviewed questions in band 5–6.5.
- **Evaluator.** Stages 1 (essay-derived observation) and 8 (transfer into a later writing task) depend on an essay
  evaluator. None has been validated:
  - the LLM evaluator has not been run;
  - the pattern stand-in is a demonstration tool, not an assessment;
  - present and past simple has no pattern rule at all.

  Until an evaluator is validated on the benchmark, essay observations can only *suggest* a gap. The diagnostic
  questions decide, which is already how the system works.

**Exactly what needs educator approval** for one complete sequence is listed under each competency above: 41
questions (19 from the supplement) and 19 lesson parts. All of them are in the consolidated review package.
