# Listen and answer

Added 19 November 2026. Practice-only; hidden in pilot builds.

- **What the child does.** The phone reads a short passage aloud. The words are not on screen. Four questions
  follow: details, order or cause, the main idea, and what can be worked out. The child may listen again at any
  time. After the last answer the passage is shown, with each right answer and the reason.
- **Reading aloud.** The passage is spoken one sentence at a time, because some phones cut long speech off.
- **Sitting.** One passage. Four right moves the level up; none or one moves it down. Passages do not repeat
  until all at the level have been heard.
- **Content.** 12 passages, 4 per level, 48 questions (`content/english/listening/passages.json`), written for
  OpenPath: stories at levels 1 and 2 (about 60 and 90 words), stories and factual pieces at level 3 (about 110).
  Rules: numbers written as words, three different choices, the right choice not the longest by far, and the right
  choice falls equally often in each position.
- **Where it shows.** A card under Practise; the home Listening row offers a passage once the day's sentences are
  written; the parent view shows questions right and the kinds missed.
- **Syllabus.** The lines on understanding what is heard now point here: Class 5 LS3 and LS4 (in the app), Class 6
  LS1 (in the app) and LS2 (partly), Class 4 LS6 (partly). Totals: Class 4 7/6/12, Class 5 14/7/12, Class 6 11/10/25.

## Limits

- Twelve passages is three or four sittings per level before repeats.
- The voice is the phone's own; its clarity on a physical phone is unchecked. The automated tests have no voice.
- The three factual pieces (the bee dance, stepwells, salt pans) were written from general knowledge and not checked
  against a cited source.
- The level starts from the level check's listening result, which came from dictation, not from passages.
