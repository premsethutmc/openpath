# Simulated English learners (sim-english@1)

8 kinds of learner, 20 of each, take Find my level and then 30 days of every English stream, one sitting of each a day. A learner's ability in a stream is hidden and on that stream's own scale; it rises a little after each sitting at or above it. Figures are averages over the 20. Behaviour only: see frontend/scripts/sim-english.mts for the numbers assumed.

Reading: "level at end" is the highest level passed, averaged over its six skills. "Placed within one level" compares Find my level's start with the hidden ability.

## The learners

- **beginner**: barely any English yet.
- **early**: reads and writes short simple sentences.
- **average**: comfortable with everyday English.
- **strong**: reads and writes well.
- **reads-but-no-grammar**: an older learner: reads and listens well, cannot name a noun.
- **uneven**: reads well, but hears and spells poorly.
- **careless**: average, but one answer in five is a slip.
- **guesser**: taps anything.
