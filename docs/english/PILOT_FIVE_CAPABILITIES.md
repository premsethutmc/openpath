**Status:** a proposal for review. Child testing is **not approved**. Nothing below has been reviewed by an educator.

### Evidence and provenance requirements (all five)

- Independent assessment: first answer, no help, fresh question, outside a lesson session.
- Practice after a lesson is shown as "improving", never "shown".
- Hints, retries and guided help are recorded as help.
- Provisional and practice-only answers are shown, never counted.
- A later check must be in a session with no lesson on that skill.

### What can be demonstrated in software now

For every capability:
- independent checks;
- the estimate with its uncertainty;
- "found hard" / "improving" / "shown at level N" / "due for a check" / "not kept";
- recommendations with their evidence;
- why the plan changed.

Teaching then a fresh check exists for spelling and grammar. For reading, a difficulty can only be handed to an adult
(no reading lessons). The prerequisite check (finding details before main idea) also works.

## Educator-review checklist

For each capability:

- [ ] Every question: one right answer; clear instructions; age-appropriate vocabulary and context; the level it is
      tagged at is plausible.
- [ ] Reading passages: suitable length, topic and cultural context; questions answerable from the passage alone;
      two questions on one passage are not near-duplicates.
- [ ] Lessons: accurate, no overgeneralised rule, age-appropriate, one clear example and one contrast.
- [ ] Hints and worked examples: labelled; do not give away answers unannounced.
- [ ] Misconception labels: describe the error accurately; parent wording is not discouraging.
- [ ] Prerequisite (main idea ← finding details): agree, disagree, or "not a strict prerequisite".
- [ ] Thresholds: agree or suggest values for "found hard", "improving", check length and a minimum gap between a
      lesson and its confirming check.
- [ ] Parent overview: each of the six distinctions is understood correctly by a parent who is not a teacher:
  - demonstrated independently;
  - improving during practice, awaiting confirmation;
  - needed help;
  - insufficient evidence;
  - due for a later check;
  - not kept at a later check.
- [ ] Sign-off recorded with name and date per item. Nothing is marked validated without it.

| Public release | Not approved |

**Realistic limits even after review:**
- At easier levels there are only 3–5 questions per skill, enough for about one check each.
- A pilot of more than one or two sessions per skill will run out of fresh questions. More reviewed questions are the
  first content need.
