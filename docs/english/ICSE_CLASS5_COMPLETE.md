## What was built

- **Six new practice topics in Grammar in use** (25 sentences): sentences in order (whole sentences as tiles),
  similes, idioms, words for cooking, sport and yoga, an opinion and its reason, words for feelings and actions.
  Each opens from its syllabus line.
- **Tasks with a grown-up** (18 tasks, `content/english/guided/tasks.json`): for the lines an app cannot mark,
  which are speaking, finding things out, and reading a book of one's own. The learner does the task aloud or
  away from the screen; an adult ticks what they saw; the ticks are kept on the learner's record
  (`guided_task` events). The screen says plainly that the app does not hear or mark these.
- **Writing lines** (four) now open the essay studio, a separate part of the app. They stay "partly": its tasks
  are not yet matched to this class.

## What "partly" still means here

- Dictation of a whole unseen passage: sentences are dictated one at a time, not a passage.
- The four writing lines, as above.

## Limits

- A tick from an adult is a record, not a measurement. It changes no level.
- The suggested literature is published work and is not in the app; the learner reads a book of their own and
  tells an adult about it.
- Classes 4 and 6 are loaded but not yet completed in this way; Classes 1 to 3, 7 and 8 are not loaded.
