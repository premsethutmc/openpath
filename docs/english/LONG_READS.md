# Long reads (long-reads v1)

*20 November 2026.*

A longer story (about 400 to 470 words) in three parts. One part a sitting: read it, then two questions with the
reason shown after each answer. After the third part, three questions on the whole story, with the whole text there
to look back at: what it is about (theme), how a character changes, and what the title means. The next sitting goes
on from where the learner stopped; a finished story is followed by a new one nearest the learner's reading level
(level 4 at the least).

Six stories, all written for OpenPath, one on each theme the Class 7 syllabus names: home and family (The Room on the
Roof, level 4), community (The Well on Gandhi Street, 4), science (The Seed Experiment, 5), the environment (Turtle
Night, 5), media (The Photograph, 6) and history (The Runner from Madras, 6). They close Class 7 R1 (texts on a wide
range of themes) and Class 8 R1 (a text's central theme and how it develops). Not reviewed by an educator.

Six more (version 2, 21 November 2026), so a reader at each level has four: friendship (The New Boy's Lunch, 4),
sport (The Last Over, 4), technology (Grandmother's Video Call, 5), celebration (The Kolam Competition, 5),
adventure (Fog on the Western Ghats, 6) and mystery and investigation (The Mystery of the Missing Sugar, 6). Same
checks: about 360 words in three parts, two questions a part, three on the whole (theme, character, title), no
right answer much longer than the wrong ones, house word screen and the speller.

Practice-only: `long_read` events on the learner's record; no level and no estimate change. Shown in the parent view
and the usage summary. Code: `frontend/src/english/logic/reading/long-read.ts`, `ui/LongRead.tsx`; content
`content/english/reading/long-reads.json`; tests `long-read.test.ts`, `e2e/long-read.test.mjs`.
