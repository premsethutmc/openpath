## What it showed (21 Nov 2026)

- The app works as designed: reviews come (about 190 review answers a year), the simpler way shows, levels move with the child, and children keep at the edge of what they can do (about 63% right).
- A beginner with no grammar, at one sitting a day on school days, masters about 14 of the 23 core topics in a year and works at level 3 of 7. A slow beginner masters about 13 but stays at levels 1–2. A child who knows the basics reaches level 4–5. Mastering a topic takes at least 40 days by design (reviews after 3, 7 and 30 days).
- Two changes were tried and not kept: at most 2 new topics a sitting (no difference), and moving up a level once 80% of its topics are learnt (faster up, fewer core topics mastered).
- Slow learners repeat many sentences (about two in three in a year) at levels 1–2: the next place to add sentences.

## Eight more, and more first sentences (21 Nov 2026)

- After a good first sitting of the day (6 or more right of 8), the done screen offers eight more as the main button (`offerMore`, GM-3). Never after a weak sitting, never a third time in a day.
- With half of those offers taken, a year's mastered topics rise: beginner 17.7 → 18.4, slow beginner 12.9 → 15.1, careless beginner 14.3 → 16.3, knows the basics 20.8 → 24.4.
- 69 more sentences for the 13 smallest level 1–2 topics (now 17–19 each; 1,192 in all), with Tamil explanations written by hand. Slow learners still repeat about two sentences in three over a year at levels 1–2: they answer about 1,500 times against about 420 sentences there, and a sentence met again months later is still practice.
