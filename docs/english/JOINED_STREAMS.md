# Joining the streams

Added 19 November 2026. What a learner gets wrong or learns in one stream comes back in another, as spelling
practice lists the app keeps up to date in My Words.

| List | Where its words come from | What each word carries |
|---|---|---|
| My tricky words | Words typed but misspelt in Listen and write, and in the level check's listening and spelling parts | The sentence it was in |
| Spell my new words | Words from New words remembered at least once after their first day | The word's own sentence |

- Both are ordinary practice lists: the word is shown first, help is on request, and a missed word returns in the
  same sitting and on later days.
- Only misspelt words are taken: a word left out, a capital letter or an end mark does not put a word on the list.
  Words under three letters are skipped.
- A list holds the 30 most recent words. A word leaves once it has been spelt right there and is no longer under
  review. The app never empties a list; old words stay until new ones replace them.
- The lists are brought up to date when a Listen and write sitting, a New words sitting or a level check finishes.
- On the home screen the Spelling row then says how many words wait and opens My Words.
- A parent can edit or delete either list like any other; the app makes it again when new words arrive.

## Not joined

- Reading to New words: only about 25 of the 400 taught words occur in the course's texts, nearly all at the
  first level, so preferring them would change almost nothing.
- A word that left a list does not come back if it is misspelt again later.
