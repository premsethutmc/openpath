## What was built

- **Eight new practice topics in Grammar in use** (34 sentences): prefixes and suffixes, verbs that take an object, sentences in order (whole sentences as tiles),
  similes, idioms, words for cooking, sport and yoga, an opinion and its reason, words for feelings and actions.
  Each opens from its syllabus line.
- **Tasks with a grown-up** (60 tasks, `content/english/guided/tasks.json`): for the lines an app cannot mark,
  which are speaking, finding things out, and reading a book of one's own. The learner does the task aloud or
  away from the screen; an adult ticks what they saw; the ticks are kept on the learner's record
  (`guided_task` events). The screen says plainly that the app does not hear or mark these.
- **Writing lines** (four in Class 4, four in Class 5, nine in Class 6) now open the essay studio, a separate part of the app. They stay "partly": its tasks
  are not yet matched to this class.

## What "partly" still means here

- Dictation of a whole unseen passage: sentences are dictated one at a time, not a passage.
- The four writing lines, as above.

## Limits

- A tick from an adult is a record, not a measurement. It changes no level.
- The suggested literature is published work and is not in the app; the learner reads a book of their own and
  tells an adult about it.
- Class 6 has 19 lines that are only partly covered: nine are writing; the rest include metaphor and personification, sorting verbs into transitive and intransitive, and reading between the lines.
- Classes 1, 2 and 8 are not loaded.
- Class 7 was read from a web page that sets out the syllabus, not from the pages of the document, so it has no page numbers and may be less complete than the others.
- Classes 3 and 7 added five practice topics (the subject of a sentence, collective and material nouns, alphabetical order, synonyms, antonyms and analogies, sound words) and 30 tasks with a grown-up (90 in all).
