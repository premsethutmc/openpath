# Advanced levels at the top of three streams (November 2026)

Status: candidate content, practice-only. Levels are OpenPath's estimates; nothing is educator-reviewed.

Levels are described by what a learner can do, never by class or age.

| Stream | New top level | What a learner does there | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading | 6 Expert | reads an argument of about 240 words: weighs the small words (only, at least, provided that), follows prior to and subsequently, finds the cause the writer accepts, reads the writer's attitude, states the argument, and tells a checked fact from a prediction or recommendation | 6 lessons, 7 texts, 36 questions |
| Listen and write | 7 Formal sentences | writes a long formal sentence with advanced spellings (conscientious, questionnaire, bureaucratic) | 24 sentences |
| Grammar in use | 7 Advanced grammar | passive, if sentences, should have and must have, -ing or to, matching forms in a list | 19 sentences |

- The texts of reading level 6 are invented reports (a library, a cycle lane, a homework club), so they hold no
  real-world facts that need a source.
- Find my level reaches the new levels from the middle: reading in three texts, listening in four sentences,
  grammar in six taps. Nothing stored had to be renumbered: the new levels were added above the old ones.

## Still missing

- **Below the present first levels.** A learner who cannot yet read a simple sentence, or does not know everyday
  words, is placed on level 1 and will find it hard. This needs its own design (pictures and sound, single
  words before sentences), not only easier text.
- **Words** stop at band 8 and **Listen and answer** at passages of about 125 words (speech on phones limits
  the length). **Spelling** is unchanged.
- Formal and academic writing beyond one level; long articles.
