Each general explanation: what you are finding → why the operation is valid (same to both sides; the bracket multiplies every term) →
the worked example, one line at a time, **each line followed by its reason** → the check in the **original** equation → "Your turn".
Each misconception explanation: the wrong answer on one question and the family's own description of the error → the fix → that question
solved correctly with a check → "Your turn". "Incorrect substitution checks" is taught in every general explanation (check in the
original equation; with x on both sides, both sides; with a bracket, work the bracket first) — it is not diagnosable from an answer, so it
has no misconception explanation.

One-step equations (B) belong to `MATH.algebra.linear-intro` (family `ahone`); they appear here as the bridge (`balance`), not as a
duplicate skill, and are not rechecked in this competency.

## Verification of the mathematics

- Teaching contract (machine): all 12 explanations `machine-checked`, 0 violations; every worked example's arithmetic claims hold and its
  result is the question's checked answer (family validators: substitution).
- Independent re-derivation (`linear-equations.test.ts`, tests 1 and 1b): every worked line of every screen, and the instance teaching of
  240 generated variants (lin, lboth, lbrk), is parsed as an equation in x and shown to have exactly the original solution (true at x,
  false at x + 1); every check line substitutes the answer into the original equation's sides; every "Not W, but A" uses the wrong answer
  the quoted misconception predicts.
