Contents

Chapter One — Numbers to 100 000 (pages 1 to 16)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• read, write and order numbers up to 100 000 (page 3)
• round a number to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000 (page 11)

Chapter Two — Times Tables and Sharing (pages 17 to 36)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• recall the times tables up to 10 × 10 quickly (page 18)
• multiply a three-digit number by a one-digit number in columns (page 24)
• share a number into equal groups and say what is left over (page 30)

Chapter Three — Parts of a Whole (pages 37 to 58)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• name a part of a shape divided into equal pieces as a fraction (page 38)
• recognise fractions that name the same amount, such as 1/2 and 2/4 (page 43)
• decide which of two fractions with the same bottom number is bigger (page 49)
• find the total of two fractions that have the same denominator (page 53)
• solve word problems involving fractions (page 57)

Chapter Four — Covering and Fencing (pages 59 to 72)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• find the space covered by a shape by counting unit squares (page 60)
• work out the distance around a rectangle (page 66)

Chapter Five — Turning and Circles (pages 73 to 84)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• identify right angles, and angles smaller or larger than a right angle (page 74)
• name the centre, radius and diameter of a circle (page 80)

Chapter Six — Timetables (pages 85 to 92)
By the end of this chapter, learners can:
• use a bus timetable to plan a short journey (page 86)
