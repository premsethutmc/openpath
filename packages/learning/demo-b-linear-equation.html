🔢 Solving: 3x + 7 = 19

Find the value of x that makes both sides equal

Meaning of Equality

The equation says: "three times some number, plus seven, equals nineteen."

Both sides must always represent the same value. Whatever we do to one side, we must do to both.

Transformation Chain

3x + 7 = 19
↓
3x = 19 − (7) = 12
↓
x = 12 ÷ 3 = 4

Step-by-Step (Before → After)

Step 1: Take 7 from both sides: 12
3x + 7 = 19 → subtract 7 on both sides → 3x = 12. The equation stays balanced because the same operation is applied to both sides.

Step 2: Divide by 3: 12 ÷ 3 = 4

3(4) + 7 = 12 + 7 = 19
